## 1. Overview

This portfolio documents my involvement in community health outreach, preventive healthcare, humanitarian service and health promotion initiatives across Ghana between 2017 and 2026.

My involvement has progressed from student-led community health initiatives to organizing and delivering physician-led healthcare interventions.

Through collaboration with healthcare professionals, academic institutions, religious organizations, professional associations, non-governmental organizations and community stakeholders, these activities have sought to bring essential healthcare services closer to communities.

Beyond clinical service delivery, my contributions have included programme organization, resource mobilization, stakeholder engagement, community education, blood donation mobilization and multidisciplinary team coordination.

These initiatives reflect my commitment to reducing barriers to healthcare access, promoting preventive medicine and improving population health outcomes.

## 4. Featured Community Health Initiatives

### 4.1 Gambaga Outcast Home Humanitarian Health Outreach

**Role:** Organizer, Team Lead and Volunteer Physician  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Location:** Gambaga, North East Region, Ghana

Conceived, organized and coordinated a humanitarian health outreach providing clinical screening, medical consultations, preventive health education and material assistance to marginalized and displaced populations.

The initiative involved engagement with traditional authorities, local healthcare personnel and community stakeholders to facilitate access to healthcare services.

The outreach screened 141 individuals, including 87 women, 40 children and 14 members of the surrounding community.

Among the 87 women screened, 29 (33.3%) had elevated blood pressure readings.

The team identified stigma-related barriers to accessing healthcare and medication refills and facilitated on-site antihypertensive medication provision.

Community education activities also addressed discrimination, preventive healthcare and appropriate healthcare-seeking behavior.

The outreach screening figures were verified by the Medical Superintendent of Gambaga.

**Selected Outputs**

- 141 individuals screened.
- 87 women and 40 children screened at the Gambaga Outcast Home.
- 14 surrounding community members screened.
- 29 women identified with elevated blood pressure.
- On-site antihypertensive medication refills facilitated.
- Traditional authorities and community stakeholders engaged.

### 4.2 School-Wide Health Screening and Mental Health Promotion

**Role:** Organizer and Health Education Speaker  
**Institution:** Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST)  
**Date:** 22 July 2023  
**Capacity:** Head, National Union of Ghana Students (NUGS) Health Committee

Organized a university-wide health screening initiative in collaboration with healthcare personnel and partner organizations to promote preventive healthcare and early identification of health concerns among students.

The screening reached 682 students.

Recognizing the importance of mental health within academic environments, I also organized and delivered a school-wide health education talk titled:

**"Beyond the Grades: Your Mental Health Matters Too."**

The talk emphasized the importance of psychological well-being alongside academic achievement, encouraging students to prioritize their mental health and seek appropriate support when necessary.

The initiative combined preventive healthcare delivery with mental health promotion, reinforcing the importance of a holistic approach to student well-being.

The programme also incorporated blood donation mobilization, bringing together health education, preventive healthcare and voluntary blood donation within a university-wide public health initiative.

**Selected Outputs**

- 682 students reached through health screening.
- School-wide mental health education delivered.
- Healthcare personnel and partner organizations mobilized.
- Student blood donation programme organized.
- Preventive healthcare and mental health awareness promoted.

## 9. Public Health Reflection

My involvement in community healthcare initiatives has reinforced the importance of addressing healthcare needs beyond the walls of hospitals.

Beginning with student-led health screening activities in 2017, my engagement has evolved to include programme organization, community mobilization, mental health promotion, surgical volunteering and physician-led humanitarian healthcare.

These experiences have demonstrated how geographical barriers, financial constraints, health literacy, stigma and fragmented healthcare delivery can influence access to essential services.

They have also reinforced the importance of community participation in public health interventions.

Organizing a school-wide screening that reached 482 students demonstrated the potential of academic institutions as platforms for preventive healthcare delivery and health promotion.

Delivering mental health education within an academic environment and leading health education at Adum Market reinforced the importance of addressing psychological well-being alongside physical health.

Participation in blood donation initiatives demonstrated how community mobilization and collaboration with non-governmental organizations can contribute to the availability of essential healthcare resources.

My engagement in breast cancer awareness, vaccination education and youth-focused sexual health education has further reinforced the importance of preventive healthcare and health literacy across different stages of life.

My humanitarian healthcare experience in Gambaga further highlighted the relationship between social exclusion, healthcare access and continuity of treatment.

While community outreach can address immediate healthcare needs, sustainable improvements require stronger primary healthcare systems, effective referral pathways, equitable resource allocation, reliable healthcare supply chains and evidence-informed health policies.

These experiences have shaped my interest in public health, health policy, epidemiology and health systems strengthening.

My long-term commitment is to contribute to healthcare systems that extend quality healthcare to underserved populations, regardless of their geographical location or socioeconomic circumstances.
